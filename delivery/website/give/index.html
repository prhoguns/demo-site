    Contact & Parish Life/Give

    Give

    Your offering keeps the lights on, the pantry full and the doors open seven days a week. Here is every way to give, and where the money goes.

    In this section

    • Contact & Office Hours
    • Give

    St. Columba receives no funding from the government or the Archdiocese. Everything — salaries, heat, the roof, the food pantry, youth ministry, the livestream — is paid for by the people who worship here. Thank you for making it possible.

    Fastest

    Give online

    One-time or monthly gifts by credit card or bank account through the Archdiocese of Toronto's secure portal. Choose "St. Columba Parish, Toronto" from the list.

    Give online

    Most reliable

    Pre-Authorized Giving

    A monthly gift drawn from your bank account on the 1st or 15th. Steady income lets the parish plan; you never have to remember an envelope.

    PAG enrolment form (PDF)Download

    Traditional

    Envelopes

    Registered parishioners receive a box of numbered envelopes each December. Cash or cheque in the collection is recorded to your name for your tax receipt.

    Register for envelopes

    From your phone

    E-transfer

    Send an Interac e-transfer to giving@stcolumbatoronto.ca. Deposits are automatic; put your envelope number or full name in the message so we can receipt it.

    Beyond the parish

    ShareLife

    The Archdiocese's annual appeal funds Catholic agencies serving the vulnerable across the GTA. Our parish goal this year is $85,000. Special collections in March, May and June.

    About ShareLife

    Lasting

    Gifts in your will

    A bequest, a gift of securities or a life insurance policy can support the parish for generations. Speak in confidence with Fr. Moreau or your advisor; we can provide suggested wording.

    Where your gift goes

    The Finance Council publishes a full annual report each spring; a summary is on the Parish Councils page and printed copies are at the back of the church. Roughly speaking, $25 a week from a family covers that family's share of heating, lighting and insurance; anything above that builds ministry.

    Give time and talent too

    Money is one gift of three. The parish runs on volunteers: lectors, counters, gardeners, pantry sorters, catechists, drivers. If your budget is tight this year, an hour a week is just as welcome.

    Questions

    Will I get a tax receipt?

    Yes. One receipt for all your gifts of the calendar year is mailed (or emailed) by the end of February. Online gifts are receipted by the Archdiocese.

    Is online giving secure?

    The portal is operated by the Archdiocese of Toronto and processes cards through a PCI-compliant provider. The parish never sees or stores your card number.

    Can I designate my gift?

    Yes: building fund, organ fund, St. Vincent de Paul, youth ministry and refugee sponsorship all accept designated gifts. Undesignated gifts go to the general operating fund, which is where the need is greatest.

    Why does the parish pay an assessment to the Archdiocese?

    The assessment supports seminary formation, retired priests, chancery services and poorer parishes. It is calculated on ordinary income and audited annually.

    Questions about giving? Contact Teresa Alvarez, Office Manager, at ext. 21 or giving@stcolumbatoronto.ca.
