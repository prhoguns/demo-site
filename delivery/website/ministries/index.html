    A parish is not a building; it is people doing things together for God and neighbour. Every group below is open to newcomers — most of them are looking for you. Contact the coordinator directly, or use the volunteer form and we will make the introduction.

    Volunteer
    Calendar of meetings

    Give an hour a week

    Roughly four hundred parishioners serve in a ministry. Most give one or two hours a week; some give a Saturday a month. All volunteers who work with children, youth, the elderly or money complete the Archdiocese's screening process, which the parish makes as painless as possible.
