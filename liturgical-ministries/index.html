    About 180 parishioners serve at the altar, the ambo, the doors and behind the scenes each week. Most serve once or twice a month on a rota published two months ahead; all are trained and commissioned by the pastor. If you attend Mass regularly, you are qualified to begin.

    Lectors

    Role: proclaim the first and second readings and the prayers of the faithful · Commitment: one Mass a month · Training: a two-hour workshop twice a year · Screening: low risk

    Clear voice, preparation at home with the readings sent a week ahead. Teens from Grade 9 welcome.

    Extraordinary Ministers of Holy Communion

    Role: assist with Communion at Mass; bring Communion to the sick · Commitment: two Masses a month · Training: archdiocesan session plus parish practice · Screening: high risk (for home visits)

    Confirmed Catholics in good standing, commissioned for three years.

    Altar Servers

    Role: serve at the altar · Ages: Grade 3 to adult · Training: three sessions · Screening: adults, low risk

    Monthly rota; weddings and funerals by sign-up.

    Altar servers

    Ushers & Greeters

    Role: welcome, seat, take up the collection, assist in emergencies · Commitment: one Mass a month · Training: one evening including first aid orientation · Screening: low risk

    Hospitality

    Sacristans

    Role: prepare vessels, vestments, books and candles before Mass; care for the sanctuary and linens · Commitment: weekly · Training: one-to-one with the head sacristan · Screening: low risk

    Art & Environment

    Role: flowers, banners and seasonal decoration · Commitment: six or seven Saturdays a year · Screening: low risk

    Gardeners and anyone with an eye for colour.

    Children's Liturgy Leaders

    Role: lead the children's Liturgy of the Word at 11:00 AM · Commitment: one Sunday a month · Training: archdiocesan course · Screening: high risk

    Children & families

    Music

    Choirs, cantors, instrumentalists

    Music ministry

    Livestream & sound

    Role: run the camera and sound desk at the 11:00 AM Mass · Commitment: one Sunday a month · Training: two sessions · Screening: low risk

    Teens and adults comfortable with technology.

    How it works

    1. Fill in the form below or speak to any priest after Mass.
    2. The ministry coordinator calls you within two weeks and invites you to the next training.
    3. Complete screening at the level shown above (a form for low-risk ministries; a police check for high-risk ones).
    4. You are added to the rota and commissioned at a weekend Mass in October or February.
