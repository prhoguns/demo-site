Devotions are the prayers that have carried Catholics through ordinary days for centuries. None of them is required; all of them are open to anyone. Pick one and come — you will be shown what to do.

Daily Rosary

Monday–Friday · after the 8:30 AM Mass · Church

Led by parishioners; the mysteries of the day. About fifteen minutes.

How to pray the rosary

Novena to Our Lady of Perpetual Help

Wednesdays · 7:30 PM after the evening Mass · Church

A parish tradition since 1962, with petitions read aloud and Benediction on the first Wednesday.

Contact: Marlene Aquino · novena@stcolumbatoronto.ca

Divine Mercy Chaplet

Fridays · 3:00 PM · Church

The Hour of Mercy. Chaplet, a short reading from St. Faustina's diary, and silence. Divine Mercy Sunday celebrated with 3:00 PM Holy Hour.

The chaplet text

First Friday & First Saturday

Monthly · Fri 7:00 PM Adoration · Sat after 9:00 AM Mass

Devotion to the Sacred Heart on First Fridays and to the Immaculate Heart on First Saturdays, with confessions available.

Adoration

Stations of the Cross

Fridays in Lent · 7:00 PM · Church (children's Stations 4:00 PM)

Led in turn by parish groups; Good Friday Stations at noon, outdoors when weather allows.

Charismatic Prayer Group

Tuesdays · 7:00 PM · Parish hall

Praise and worship, Scripture sharing and prayer for healing, in English and Spanish. Life in the Spirit seminar each autumn.

Contact: Carlos Mendoza · prayergroup@stcolumbatoronto.ca

Padre Pio Prayer Group

3rd Sunday · after the 1:00 PM Italian Mass · Chapel

Rosary and prayers of St. Pio in Italian and English; Mass for the feast on September 23.

Contact: Giuseppe Rossi · padrepio@stcolumbatoronto.ca

Bukas Loob sa Diyos

2nd Sunday · after the Tagalog Mass · Parish hall

Filipino Catholic community prayer and fellowship; Simbang Gabi novena Masses in December.

Cultural communities

Legion of Mary

Mondays · 7:00 PM · Meeting room B

Prayer and active works — visiting the sick, the housebound and newcomers.

Legion of Mary

Christian Meditation

Thursdays · 7:30 PM · Chapel

Thirty minutes of silent meditation in the tradition of John Main, with a short teaching. Beginners welcome.

Contact: Helen Park · meditation@stcolumbatoronto.ca

Liturgy of the Hours

Daily · Morning Prayer 8:10 AM before Mass

The Church's daily prayer of psalms; books provided. Evening Prayer sung on Sundays of Advent and Lent at 4:30 PM.

Prayer chain

Any time · by email

Sixty parishioners who pray for requests the same day they are received.

Send a request

Votive candles

Candles may be lit at the shrine of Our Lady and at the St. Columba shrine any time the church is open. A suggested offering of $2 covers the cost. The sanctuary lamp, which burns beside the tabernacle, may be offered for an intention for one week; see Mass intentions.

Starting a new group

If a group of parishioners would like to gather for a devotion not listed here — a language rosary, a Bible-sharing circle, a men's or women's prayer breakfast — speak to Fr. Nguyen. New groups need a named coordinator who has completed volunteer screening, and a room booked through the office.
