"For I was hungry and you gave me food." Everything on this page is a parish's answer to that sentence. Most of it happens in the hall, on Saturday mornings, and in the homes of neighbours you will never read about — and every part of it needs hands.

Out of the Cold

Wednesday nights · November to March · parish hall

A hot supper, a warm bed and breakfast for up to 40 guests experiencing homelessness, in partnership with neighbouring churches and a downtown agency. Volunteer shifts: cooking (2–5 PM), supper and hospitality (5–9 PM), overnight (9 PM–7 AM, in pairs), breakfast and cleanup (6–8 AM).

Contact: Joan Petrie · ootc@stcolumbatoronto.ca

St. Vincent de Paul

Food pantry Tue & Sat 10–12 · home visits

Groceries for ninety households a week and practical help with rent, utilities and furniture.

Three ways to help

  • Time. A pantry morning, an Out of the Cold shift, a drive to a refugee family's medical appointment. Sign up on the volunteer page; most roles need only low-risk screening.
  • Money. Designate a gift on the giving page to St. Vincent de Paul, Out of the Cold or refugee sponsorship. Every dollar goes to the program.
  • Prayer. The petitions at every Mass name the poor, the homeless, refugees and prisoners; the prayer chain prays for our guests by first name.

Why the Church does this

Catholic social teaching rests on the dignity of every person, made in God's image; on the common good; on solidarity with the poor; and on care for creation. It is not a political program but a way of seeing. The parish's Justice & Peace committee offers a four-week introduction each autumn, and the Archdiocese's Office for Refugees and its social justice office provide resources for parish groups.

I have a criminal record. Can I volunteer?

Often, yes. Talk to the screening coordinator in confidence; many roles do not require a police check, and a record is not an automatic bar.

Can my company or school group volunteer as a team?

Yes — pantry sorting and Out of the Cold cooking work well for groups of six to ten. Email the office two weeks ahead.
