Most of us stopped learning about our faith at the age of twelve, and then tried to live an adult life on a child's understanding. Adult formation is where that changes. Everything here is free, nothing requires a background, and questions are the point.

Alpha

Thursdays 7:00 – 9:00 PM · parish hall · ten weeks from September 24, plus a Saturday away-day

A free meal, a short film, and an honest small-group conversation about life, faith and meaning. Designed for people exploring — bring a friend who has never set foot in a church. Registration helps with catering but drop-ins are welcome. Also offered in Spanish in January.

Contact: Sam & Teresa Whitfield · alpha@stcolumbatoronto.ca

Bible Study

1st & 3rd Thursdays 7:00 PM · meeting room A · September to June

This year we read the Gospel of Luke, a few chapters at a time, with a short video from a Scripture scholar and discussion. Bring any Bible; a study guide is $20. No preparation needed.

Leader: Deacon Paul Ferreira

Advent & Lent series

Four Wednesday evenings · after the 7:00 PM Mass

A guest speaker each season: recent series on the Mass, on prayer, on the saints of Canada, and on Catholic social teaching.

FORMED

Online, any time · free with the parish subscription

Thousands of Catholic films, courses, talks and children's programs. Go to formed.org, choose "Sign up as a parishioner", find St. Columba Toronto, and you are in.

Book club

Last Monday 7:30 PM · parish library

One book a month — spiritual classics, Catholic novels, a bishop's letter. Titles for the year are on the noticeboard.

Speaker nights

Three or four a year

Evenings with a theologian, a missionary, a Catholic doctor or lawyer on a live question. Wine and cheese.

Other paths

  • Adults seeking baptism, reception or confirmation: OCIA.
  • Parents preparing children for sacraments learn alongside them: programs.
  • Want to teach? Become a catechist.
  • The Archdiocese's Formation for Discipleship office offers courses and certificates; ask Sr. Agnes.
  • Daily Scripture at Living with Christ; the Catechism online at the Vatican website.

Questions

I am not Catholic. Can I come?

Yes to all of it. Alpha in particular was made for you.

I work evenings.

Bible study is recorded for members; FORMED is on your schedule; and a Saturday-morning Alpha runs in Lent.

Is there childcare?

At Alpha, yes (register in advance). For other evenings, ask — we can usually arrange it.

Coordinator: Sr. Agnes Ibeh, SSND — ext. 25, faith@stcolumbatoronto.ca.
