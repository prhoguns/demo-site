The years between leaving home and settling down are when many Catholics drift — not from anger, but because nobody noticed them leave. Our young adult community exists so that does not happen here. About sixty people take part; a dozen of them are on the leadership team with Marcus Lee and Fr. Nguyen.

Sunday 5:00 PM Mass & supper

Weekly · supper in the hall most Sundays, $5

The easiest way in. Sit anywhere; stay for food afterwards. The contemporary ensemble leads the music and needs singers and players.

Theology on Tap

Last Wednesday · 7:30 PM · a nearby pub or the hall

A speaker, a question, a drink, a real conversation. Recent topics: faith and mental health, Catholic social teaching and housing, dating with intention.

Bible study

Thursdays · 7:30 PM · meeting room A (after the parish Bible study)

Small-group lectio on the coming Sunday's Gospel. Forty-five minutes; no homework.

Service

Monthly Saturday

Out of the Cold cooking team in winter, pantry sorting, Habitat builds, refugee family move-ins.

Retreats & pilgrimages

February retreat · summer pilgrimage

A silent weekend in February; in summer a bus to Martyrs' Shrine in Midland or the Oratory in Montreal.

Socials

Every few weeks

Trivia nights, hikes, skating at Nathan Phillips Square, a Christmas party. Newcomers to Toronto especially welcome.

On campus

Students at U of T, TMU, York and the colleges have Catholic chaplaincies of their own — the Newman Centre, campus ministries and residence chaplains — and we work alongside them. Many students worship at St. Columba on Sundays and on campus during the week. See chaplaincies for contacts. If you are in Toronto for a year on exchange or a work placement, you belong here for that year.

Faith, vocation, life

Some of the community are discerning marriage, priesthood or religious life; some are exploring the Church for the first time and join the OCIA group; some are engaged and preparing for marriage; some have just come back after years away and start with confession. Fr. Nguyen offers spiritual direction to young adults on Tuesday evenings by appointment.

Join

Email youngadults@stcolumbatoronto.ca or message Marcus after the 5:00 PM Mass and you will be added to the group chat and the fortnightly email. There is no membership and no fee. Coordinators this year: Priya Nair and Daniel Osei.

I'm older than 35 / younger than 18.

Under 18, see youth ministry. Over 35, everyone is welcome at Theology on Tap and service days; the parish's other groups may also fit.

I don't know anyone.

Nor did anyone else the first week. Tell the person at the supper table you are new; that is what the table is for.
