Getting here

By TTC

Line 2 to Pape Station, then the 25 Don Mills bus north to Columba Avenue (about six minutes), or an eight-minute walk. The 81 Thorncliffe Park bus stops two blocks east.

By car

Free parking behind the hall (entrance off Columba Avenue, 60 spaces, 4 accessible) and three-hour street parking on Sundays. Please leave the spaces nearest the accessible entrance for those who need them.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance on the east side, elevator to the hall, accessible washrooms, hearing loop and large-print missals. Full details.
