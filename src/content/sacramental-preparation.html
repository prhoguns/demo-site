Sacramental preparation is coordinated by Sr. Agnes Ibeh, SSND, Pastoral Associate and Catechetical Coordinator, with a team of twenty-two trained volunteer catechists. Programs are parish-based: children from Catholic, public and home schools prepare together, and families are involved at every step.

Key dates 2026–27

Become a catechist

Catechists lead small groups of children or adults through the sessions, using materials the parish provides. No degree is needed: a living faith, patience, and two hours a month. New catechists shadow an experienced one for a year, complete the Archdiocese's volunteer screening (this is a high-risk ministry, so a police check is required) and attend the diocesan catechist formation day each August. Former teachers, parents of past candidates and confirmed high-school students (as assistants) make up most of the team.

Questions

We missed the registration deadline.

Call Sr. Agnes. Late registrations are accepted until the first session when space allows; otherwise the child joins the following year.

What does it cost?

First Communion $40, Confirmation $60, OCIA free, marriage preparation $150 per couple, baptism preparation free. All fees are waived on request.

Our family speaks Spanish / Tagalog / Italian at home.

Sessions are in English with bilingual catechists in each group; family books are available in Spanish and Tagalog. Parent meetings are interpreted on request.

My child has special needs.

Every child can receive the sacraments. Tell us what helps your child learn and we will adapt — a one-to-one catechist, shorter sessions, visual materials, or preparation at home with our support.
