Submit an announcement

Parish groups and ministries may submit announcements for the bulletin. Please email bulletin@stcolumbatoronto.ca by Wednesday at noon for the coming weekend, with:

  • a title and no more than 60 words of text (we edit for length);
  • the date, time and place, and a contact name;
  • whether it should also appear on the website calendar.

Announcements for outside organizations are printed at the pastor's discretion, and only for events consistent with Catholic teaching. Commercial notices are not accepted, but see advertising below.

Advertising

The back page of the bulletin carries advertisements from local businesses, which cover the full cost of printing. Spaces are sold annually each June; a business-card space is $360 per year, and parishioner-owned businesses receive priority. Contact the office manager at ext. 21.

Also in every bulletin

  • The Sunday readings and a reflection from the pastor
  • Names of the sick and the recently deceased (with permission)
  • Banns of marriage
  • Offertory totals and ShareLife progress
  • Archdiocesan notices
