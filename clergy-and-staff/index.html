    Clergy & Staff

    The priests, deacon and staff who serve St. Columba, with the quickest way to reach the right person.

    In this section

    • Who We Are

    A parish of 1,200 families is served by a small team. Two priests, a deacon, a religious sister and five lay staff keep the sacraments celebrated, the phones answered, the choir rehearsed and the boiler running. Each person is listed below with what they look after, so that your call or email lands with the right person the first time. The office number for everyone is 416-555-0142, followed by the extension.

    Clergy

    Rev. Daniel Moreau

    Pastor

    Fr. Moreau was ordained for the Archdiocese of Toronto in 2003 and came to St. Columba in 2019 after serving in Scarborough and Mississauga. He is responsible for the whole life of the parish: liturgy, finances, staff, and the pastoral plan. He also chairs the Pastoral Council and celebrates most weddings and funerals.

    pastor@stcolumbatoronto.ca · ext. 22

    Rev. Joseph Nguyen

    Associate Pastor

    Fr. Nguyen was ordained in 2021 and joined us in 2024. He works closely with the youth and young adult ministry, the OCIA process, and the Tagalog and Spanish communities, and he celebrates the 5:00 PM Sunday Mass. He is also the priest to ask about hospital visits and Anointing of the Sick.

    office@stcolumbatoronto.ca · ext. 23

    Deacon Paul Ferreira

    Permanent Deacon

    Deacon Paul was ordained in 2015 and has served here ever since. He is a retired TTC supervisor, married to Fatima, with three grown children. He prepares parents for baptism, leads the bereavement ministry, preaches regularly, and coordinates the Portuguese community.

    office@stcolumbatoronto.ca · ext. 24

    Office hours

    Parish office

    Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM (closed 12:00–1:00 for lunch). Closed on statutory holidays and from December 24 to January 1. The office is on the ground floor of the rectory, to the right of the church; ring the bell. Outside these hours, leave a message at 416-555-0142 and we will return your call the next business day. For a priest in an emergency, such as a parishioner who is dying, choose option 9 on the phone menu at any hour.

    Who to contact for what

    All email addresses end in @stcolumbatoronto.ca. Concerns about the conduct of clergy or staff may be raised directly with the Archdiocese; see reporting misconduct.

    Considering priesthood or religious life?

    Both of our priests are happy to talk with anyone, of any age, who wonders whether God might be calling them to serve the Church. Start a conversation after Mass, or read our vocations page.
