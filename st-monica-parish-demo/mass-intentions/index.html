    Mass Intentions!

    *If you would like to offer a Mass on a open day, please contact the Parish Office by calling 416-483-1513 or by filling this Mass Intentions form https://forms.gle/rY6ftkC8dqfD9aDv9 and filling an envelope to drop off your offertory.

    Multiple Mass Intentions are now accepted on Wednesday's & Fridays! (Read Below)

    How to Book a Mass Intention on Multiple Intention Days (Wednesdays & Fridays): On Wednesdays and Fridays, multiple Mass Intentions may be offered during the same Mass.

    Each person may book one Mass Intention per Mass, with up to 3 names maximum included in that intention.

    If you wish to include more than 3 names, an additional Mass Intention must be booked for a different date.

    This 3-name limit applies to All Masses.

    Example of one Mass Intention (3 names maximum): + Ja ne, John & Mary Doe.

    This counts as one Mass Intention for one designated Mass.

    Example of multiple intentions during the same Mass: Wednesday, March 25 — + Jane, John & Mary Doe, requested by Joseph Doe; Bob Moss (Thanksgiving), requested by Margaret Moss; Billy & Marcus Ruth (Wedding Anniversary), requested by the Ruth family.

    Please separate multiple names with commas ( ie. John, Paul & Luke , not John Paul Luke as this would be treated as one name, rather than 3). Please include first and last names only when submitting intentions, if applicable (ie. John Doe, not John Patrick Lawrence Smith Doe). Please also clearly indicate whether your request is for one name or multiple names (up to 3).

    We also accept singular Weekend Mass Intentions for 2/3 of the Sunday Masses! the 5pm (Saturday Vigil) / 9am Sunday Morning will alternate as our Pro Populo Mass.

    Please book your Mass Intention at least a week in advance. Intentions recieved last minute will not be considered.
