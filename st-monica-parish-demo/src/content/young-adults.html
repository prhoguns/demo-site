Young Adult & Other Ministries

St. Monica's Parish is blessed to have many volunteers also serving in these Ministries:

Young Adults Group

Called to praise God & serve with our talents. This is a space to grow in faith, pray together, and build lasting friendships rooted in Jesus Christ. Let’s walk together on this journey of faith, hope, and love. To join: please refer to 'Young Adults' on the mainpage.

Our YA's in Action!

Ministers of Hospitality

"Contribute to the needs of the saints, practice hospitality" Romans 12:13, RSV-CE St.

Monica's Parish strives to be a welcoming community ensuring each person experiences the parish as their home away from home and finds within it a family united as brothers and sisters in common worship.

Greeters and Ushers exemplify this mission for the community by helping to establish a climate of welcome, helpfulness and order among those gathered.

They assist the assembly with practical details so that the Liturgy and Sacred Rites can be celebrated with ease and grace.

Offering Collectors

"Each of you must give as you have made up your mind, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." 2 Corinthians 9:7, NRSV. During the Mass, volunteers in this ministry collect donations then bring up the community offering to the altar.

Garden Ministry

St. Monica's Parish is blessed with a dedicated group of garden volunteers who work tirelessly in the Spring and Fall to maintain the parish gardens. Their selfless work saves the parish landscaping costs. If you would like to serve the parish through this ministry, please contact the parish office.
