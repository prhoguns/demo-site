Our Schools

St. Monica Elementary School

The mission of St.

Monica Catholic Elementary School is to educate students to their full potential.

This is achieved by providing a safe and welcoming learning environment that is an example of a Christian community, by the integration of Gospel Values into the total learning experience and by providing continual feedback on students’ progress.

St.

Monica Catholic Elementary School seeks to provide a Catholic education that will enable our students to become responsible, self-disciplined, life-long learners and members of a caring local and global community.

Marshall McLuhan Secondary School

Marshall McLuhan Catholic Secondary School’s mission follows in the traditions of a great contemporary thinker. As Marshall McLuhan did, Marshall McLuhan Catholic Secondary School strives to embody the highest goals of the Catholic intellectual traditions using our faith to understand our search for oneness, goodness and truth.
