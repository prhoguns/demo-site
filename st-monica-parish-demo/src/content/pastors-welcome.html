Welcome to St. Monica's Parish!

My dear friends, brothers and sisters in Christ!

It is an honor, and with great delight that I personally welcome all of you to our lovely parish of St. Monica, the Mother Church of Mid-Toronto, located on Yonge Street and Eglinton Avenue!

If you are living in this location or new to the area, I hope that you will be able to join us in our daily Masses and weekend Eucharistic celebrations, where we gather to sing praises and offer prayers of thanksgiving to God, to worship and adore Him, to receive the sacrament of reconciliation, and to be nourished by His body, blood, soul, and divinity.

As members of God’s family, we are also called to love and serve one another.

We can express our love for God by being charitable, kind, and compassionate to our brothers and sisters in Christ.

If you haven’t taken this step, you are most welcome to “come and see” as Jesus himself invited those who were to become His disciples.

Feel free to refer to our parish bulletin or this website for Mass schedules. I look forward to meeting you all after the weekend Masses or in the office. If there is anything that you need or do not see on this website, please feel free to contact us or any member of our staff to further assist you.

In Christ,

Fr. Joseph Baal
