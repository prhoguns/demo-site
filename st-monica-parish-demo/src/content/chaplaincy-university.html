Campus ministry in the Archdiocese of Toronto has a transformative impact on the lives of hundreds of university and college students every year.

Through campus ministry, they learn more about the Catholic faith and how to apply it in the challenges of daily student life.

They develop personally and spiritually, and make life-long friends.

In order to reach even more students, campus ministry needs financial support.

We invite you to partner with us in this important work of evangelization on campus.
