--- title: Privacy section: Our Parish description: Privacy information for the St. Monica's Parish demo website. lede: How this demo handles parish contact links, forms and analytics. ---

This is a demonstration website prepared for planning purposes. Production privacy wording should be reviewed by St. Monica's Parish and the Archdiocese of Toronto before launch.

Contact Forms

The static demo does not process contact form submissions. A future WordPress site can use an approved form tool or parish email workflow.

External Services

Some links point to trusted external services such as Google Maps, Archdiocese of Toronto resources, online giving, Google Forms or Daily Readings.

Production Review

Before publishing this demo as a live parish website, confirm the final privacy policy, analytics configuration, cookie notice, media permissions and form handling with the parish.
