Parish Announcements & Resources (Need to Know's)

Updated Announcements and Resources of the Parish:

Weekly Announcements:

We will be having Coffee Socials after the Sunday 9am & 11am Masses weekly, please be most welcome to come and stop by to enjoy some coffee and light refreshments! A donation for the maintainence fund to support this project is greatly appreciated!

RCIA is on Tuesdays from 7:30pm - 8:30pm in the Rectory Basement, please contact Peter Gavin-Griffin: pete.gavin.griffin@mail.utoronto.ca and register using the online google form, once filled, contact the Parish Office. Registration for other Sacraments will be announced in the coming weeks.

New Parish Groups to join the Parish! Stay tuned for more news!

Young Adults is every 1st and 3rd Wednesday of the Month at 7pm, feel free to join us, please contact Sydel and Maria for more information!

Good Shepherd Collection: On the First Friday of each month, donations of non-perishable food, hygiene products, new socks, and new clothing will be collected during and after Eucharistic Adoration for Good Shepherd Ministries. (First Friday Adoration is this Sept 4th at 7pm)

Our Pastor, Fr. Joseph will be away starting the 3rd week of September to the 2nd week of October, please contact the parish office with Church related inquiries!

Mass Intentions are available to book for the months of Late September to December! Forgetting an anniversary or want to remember a deceased loved one, book a Mass Intention by visiting the Parish Office!
