Our parish

A Broadway Avenue parish with deep roots

St. Monica's is home to approximately 800 families from diverse and multicultural backgrounds.

The parish belongs to the Archdiocese of Toronto, celebrates Mass daily, and continues a local story that began in 1906 through the generosity of Eugene O'Keefe.
