    Watch Mass Online

    The 11:00 AM Sunday Mass and the 8:30 AM weekday Mass are streamed live on our YouTube channel, and stay available afterwards.

    If illness, age, distance or a newborn keeps you at home, you can still pray with your parish. Our Sunday 11:00 AM Mass and the weekday 8:30 AM Mass are streamed live, and each recording remains on our channel for a month.

    How to watch

    1. On this page

      The player above switches to the live stream a few minutes before Mass begins. Press play; if it says "waiting", refresh the page at the start time.

    2. On YouTube

      Open the parish YouTube channel (demo link) and choose the "Live" tab. Subscribe and turn on notifications to be told when a stream starts.

    3. On a television

      Open the YouTube app on a smart TV, Apple TV, Roku or Chromecast, search for the channel name and choose the live video. Larger text and a bigger picture make this the best option for older parishioners.

    Praying along at home

    Set the room up as you would a pew: sit up, put the phone away, light a candle if you can. Say the responses out loud. The readings for the day are at Living with Christ. At Communion, when you cannot receive, pray the Act of Spiritual Communion:

    My Jesus, I believe that you are present in the Most Holy Sacrament. I love you above all things, and I desire to receive you into my soul. Since I cannot at this moment receive you sacramentally, come at least spiritually into my heart. I embrace you as if you were already there and unite myself wholly to you. Never permit me to be separated from you. Amen.

    Act of Spiritual Communion, St. Alphonsus Liguori

    If you are unable to come to church for a long period, please tell the office: a Lay Pastoral Visitor can bring Holy Communion to your home each week.

    Questions

    The video says "This live stream is offline."

    The stream usually starts five minutes before Mass. If it is still offline at the start time, refresh the page; if the problem continues, a recording will be posted within the hour.

    Does watching online fulfil the Sunday obligation?

    No — the obligation is to take part in Mass in person. Those who are sick, caring for the sick, or otherwise unable to attend are excused from the obligation, and the livestream is offered for them.

    Can we stream a funeral or wedding for relatives overseas?

    Yes. Ask when you arrange the celebration; there is no charge, and the link can be private.

    Will I appear on camera?

    The camera shows the sanctuary and the back of the congregation from a distance. If you prefer not to be visible at all, sit in the side pews, which are out of frame.

    On the WordPress version of this site the video is a standard YouTube embed block that the office can point at any video or live stream.
